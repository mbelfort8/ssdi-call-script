Missed-Call Opener (Callbacks)

As I can see, you currently have a disability or medical condition that has kept you from working; is that correct?

Note: Then continue with the 1st Question.
1st Question

I was calling to confirm that you’re currently not working at all, correct?

Note: Must be completely out of work. Even part-time work counts as working.
IF YES = Q  |  IF NO = NQ
2nd Question

And that’s because of a disability or medical condition that has kept you out of work for at least the past 5 months; is that correct?

Note: Must be out of work because of a medical condition for 5 continuous months or more.
YES = Q  |  NO or < 5 mos = NQ
3rd Question

Just to confirm that your age is between 50 and 65, correct?

YES = Q  |  Under 50 or over 65 = NQ
4th Question

Just so I have it right, which state are you currently living in?

Note: California (CA) and Colorado (CO) are bad states.
ANY OTHER STATE = Q  |  CA or CO = NQ
5th Question

Alright, and within the last 10 years, you’ve worked for at least 5 years, even if on and off, where taxes were deducted, correct?

Note: Doesn’t have to be 5 straight years; can be on and off. Full-time or part-time counts as long as taxes were paid (Social Security Tax). Must be legal, on-the-books work.
If unsure, ask how many years they have paid taxes in the last 10 years.
YES = Q  |  NO = NQ
6th Question

Perfect, and you’re currently seeing a doctor or specialist on a regular basis for your condition, correct?

Note: Must be under current, regular care. Treatment that ended in the past does not count.
YES = Q  |  NO = NQ
7th Question

Just to confirm, your condition is not related to any current drug or alcohol use, correct?

Note: Active substance abuse that contributes to the disability is a disqualifier.
NOT RELATED = Q  |  RELATED / ACTIVE USE = NQ
8th Question

Just to confirm, you don’t have an attorney or representative with Social Security, and you’ve never had one on a disability claim, correct?

Note: We are referring to any appointed attorney or representative on the SSDI claim, current or previous.
NO ATTORNEY = Q  |  HAS OR HAD AN ATTORNEY = NQ
